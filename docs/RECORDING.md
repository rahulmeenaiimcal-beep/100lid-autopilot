# Recording your own clips ("Rahul Sir's Exam Tip")

Your face and voice in the videos make the channel personal, and they're strong proof for YouTube's review that real teaching goes into every video.

## How it works
1. **Around the 20th of every month** you get an email titled **"🎥 Recording list for <next month>"**. It lists next month's long videos in order, with a short script for each (about 1 minute).
2. You **record them all in one sitting** (about 15–20 minutes) on your phone.
3. You **upload them** using the link in that email.
4. When the robot makes each video, it puts **that topic's clip** right after the Exam Corner and before the quiz, with your name on screen and Hindi + English subtitles taken from what you actually said.
5. Each clip is used **once**. A missing clip is fine: that video is simply made without it.

## Recording tips (phone)
- **Landscape** (phone sideways), at **eye level**, about an arm's length away. Look into the lens.
- **Light in front of you**: face a window or a lamp. Avoid a bright window behind you.
- **Quiet room**, plain or tidy background. Wired earphones with a mic sound better than the phone mic.
- Use the phone's normal camera at **1080p, 30 fps** (4K isn't needed).
- **Speak naturally.** You don't need to read word-for-word: the robot listens to your clip and writes the subtitles from your own words.
- Leave **one second of silence** at the start and end of each clip.
- Made a mistake? Just record that clip again.

## Naming and uploading
1. Name the files exactly as the email says: **tip-01**, **tip-02**, … (any video type works: .mp4, .mov…).
2. Open the **upload link** in the email (it opens a GitHub "release" page for that month).
3. Drag all the files into the box **"Attach binaries by dropping them here"**. Wait until every file has finished uploading.
4. Click the green **Publish release** button.

From a phone you can also open the link in the browser, tap **"Attach binaries"** and choose the videos.

## Your intro and outro (optional, record once)
- **intro**: 3–5 seconds, e.g. "नमस्ते, मैं राहुल। चलिए शुरू करते हैं!" (plays right after each video's opening).
- **outro**: 5–8 seconds, e.g. "ऐसे ही exam tips के लिए 100LID से जुड़े रहिए।"
- Upload them once at: **github.com/rahulmeenaiimcal-beep/100lid-autopilot/releases/new?tag=clips-brand**, named **intro** and **outro**.
- They're used on every video. To change them later, open that release, delete the old file, and add the new one.

> Note: the repository is public, so uploaded clips can be downloaded by anyone who finds the page. They'll be on YouTube anyway, but don't upload anything private.
