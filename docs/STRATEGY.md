# 100LID — Month-1 strategy (from 27 Oct 2026)

## Where the channel started
- 1,698 subscribers (enough ✅). Watch hours in the last 12 months: 87 of 4,000 ❌. No uploads in the last 90 days.
- What worked before: Hindi explainers of government schemes and banking laws (top 10 videos, 3K–16K views each), found through **YouTube search** (61% of views).
- What didn't: off-topic series (Ramayan, quizzes, podcast), Shorts (75% swiped away), very short videos (average 1 min 38 s watched).

## The plan
**One topic area only:** exam-prep explainers on government schemes, banking laws, and RBI & economy, plus one monthly banking current-affairs roundup.

| | Long videos | Shorts |
|---|---|---|
| How many | 3 a week (12 a month) | 1 a day (~30 a month) |
| When (IST) | Tue 6 pm, Thu 6 pm, Sat 11 am | Every day 8 pm |
| Length | 8–12 minutes | 40–55 seconds |
| Job | Earn watch hours (only long videos count) | Bring new viewers to the long videos |

**Monthly topic mix:** 5 schemes · 2 banking laws · 4 RBI & economy · 1 current affairs. Remakes of old hits come first: SSY, NRLM, NULM, CGTMSE, RBI Act, BR Act, Partnership Act, LAF, IRAC/NPA.

**Every long video follows one shape** (a different story each time):
hook (a character's real problem + exploded-view teaser) → chapters with story scenes and explainer boards → Exam Corner → 5-question quiz with countdown → recap ("Screenshot लें") → next-video teaser.

**Visual moments** are placed where viewers usually leave (around 25%, 50% and 70%): knolling (documents/items laid out), exploded view (parts of a whole), cutaway (what's inside), anatomy dissection (labelled cheque/form/passbook/card), plus big-number stats, timelines, comparisons and steps. No more than ~60 seconds pass without a change of scene.

**Characters:** Guruji and Meera Ma'am (teachers), Chotu (aspirant), Ramesh Kaka (farmer), Sunita Didi (mother/SHG member), Sharma ji (shopkeeper). They rotate by topic, each with their own voice.

**Every video gets:** Hindi + English subtitles, chapters, an English title and description for English-language viewers, an honest click-worthy thumbnail, 15–25 search tags, the official sources, a pinned quiz comment, and a playlist for its series.

**Staying within YouTube's rules (inauthentic-content policy, July 2026):**
every script is original and story-led, each fact is checked by a second AI against official sources, nothing is published without your OK, and nothing is mass-produced or copied.

## Month 2: your own clips (built in)
From month 2 the robot sends you a **recording list around the 20th**: next month's topics in order, each with a ~1-minute "Rahul Sir's Exam Tip" script (memory trick, most-asked fact, common mistake). You record them in one sitting and upload them; each clip goes into its own video, before the quiz, with exact subtitles from your speech. An optional intro and outro, recorded once, are used on every video as branding. See `docs/RECORDING.md`.
Each tip clip is used only once, on purpose: the same clips repeated across many videos is exactly the "repetitive" pattern YouTube refuses to pay for.
